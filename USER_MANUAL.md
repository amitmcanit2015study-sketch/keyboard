## 1. Quick Setup & Activation

Setting up Bharat Indic Keyboard takes less than 30 seconds:

1. **Open the App**: Tap on the **Bharat Indic Keyboard** app icon on your home screen or app drawer (look for the proud Indian Tricolor icon with the Ashoka Chakra).
2. **Step 1 - Enable in Settings**: Tap **"Enable Keyboard"**. In the Android Input Method settings screen, turn the toggle **ON** for *Bharat Indic Keyboard*. Tap *OK* if prompted.
3. **Step 2 - Select Active Input Method**: Tap **"Select Keyboard"** and choose *Bharat Indic Keyboard* as your default keyboard.
4. **Step 3 - Ready to Type!**: You will see a green checkmark indicating setup is complete. You can test typing right inside the app's test box!

## 3. Language Switching (English & Indic)

You have two convenient ways to switch languages:

1. **Tap the Top-Left Language Pill**: Tap the `[HN]` / `[EN]` pill on the candidate strip. It instantly toggles between your primary Indian language (e.g. Hindi) and English.
2. **Long-press Space Bar**: Long-press the Space bar to open the quick language switcher dialog to choose Devanagari, Bengali, Telugu, Tamil, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi, Urdu, or English.

## 4. Phonetic Transliteration (Hinglish to Hindi)

You do not need to memorize complex Indian script layouts! Simply type the word using standard English letters as it sounds:

| What you type | What appears in suggestions | Output |
|---|---|---|
| `namaste` | `नमस्ते` | **नमस्ते** |
| `bharat` | `भारत` | **भारत** |
| `kya hal hai` | `क्या हाल है` | **क्या हाल है** |
| `shubh ratri` | `शुभ रात्रि` | **शुभ रात्रि** |
| `dhanyawad` | `धन्यवाद` | **धन्यवाद** |

### Tips:
- **Capital Letters**: If you type with a capital letter (e.g. `India`), suggestions maintain capital formatting.
- **Space Bar Confirmation**: Hitting the space bar confirms and types the selected suggestion automatically.
- **Numbers & Punctuation**: Typing commas, periods, or numbers automatically finalizes your active Indic word.

## 5. Voice Typing (English & Hindi)

Speak naturally and watch your words appear in full sentences!

1. Tap the **Microphone `[🎤]`** icon located on the right side of the candidate strip.
2. **Speak in English**: When the keyboard is in **EN** mode, speak in English (e.g. *"I will reach the office in ten minutes"*). The entire sentence will be typed out cleanly.
3. **Speak in Hindi**: When the keyboard is in **HN** mode, speak in Hindi (e.g. *"मैं दस मिनट में ऑफिस पहुंच जाऊंगा"*). It will transcribe directly in Hindi!
4. Tap the microphone again (or press any key) to stop listening.

> **Note**: On first use, Android will ask for microphone permission. Tap "While using the app" to enable voice typing.

## 7. Customization & Settings

Open the app or tap the **`[⚙]`** settings icon on the candidate strip to customize:

## 8. Privacy & Security FAQs

### Q: Does the keyboard record my passwords or bank details?
**A: Absolutely not.**
When you enter a password or PIN field, the keyboard detects the sensitive input type and **automatically disables suggestions, word learning, and voice input**.

### Q: Can this keyboard send my typing data to the internet?
**A: No.**
Bharat Indic Keyboard has **ZERO internet permissions** (`android.permission.INTERNET` is completely absent from the app). It is physically impossible for the app to send your data to any server. Everything runs 100% locally on your smartphone.

### Q: Why does Android show a warning when enabling third-party keyboards?
**A: This is a standard system warning from Android for all third-party keyboards.**
Because our app requests no internet access, your keystrokes remain completely private on your device.
